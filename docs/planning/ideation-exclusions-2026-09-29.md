The first Muse/Luna duel found only refinements of existing Beads or additional measurements without a new product decision. Do not recycle an idea by renaming it, concatenating two entries, adding another threshold/chart/score, or putting a new gate around an existing trial. Preserve useful old guards in existing work; exclude them as the *answer* to this research request.

## Two additional first-round blindspots, also excluded as standalone answers

- **Muse: synthetic prompt-injection/tool-output obedience trials.** Testing whether an agent obeys hostile text in a file, tool result or recalled memory is important, but merely adding attack fixtures and a second PASS/FAIL scorer is not the requested new mechanism. Investigate actual attacker/defender separation, enforceable trust boundaries, and a concrete untrusted-checkout permission decision in external source before proposing anything new. Do not execute destructive payloads.
- **Luna: whole-workspace collateral-mutation detector.** Snapshotting before/after state and failing an otherwise-correct edit that changes a protected path is a useful existing-oracle extension, not a standalone product. Luna explicitly rejected it for novelty. A genuinely new proposal must do more than add an allowed-effects manifest or diff checker.

## Cross-cutting near-duplicate classes to reject

- **Measurement garnish:** a fresh latency percentile, phase diagram, cost formula, throughput plot, uncertainty method, or token/call-count visualization over the same two read/edit tasks is not a new product outcome.
- **Oracle repackaging:** another reference solution, nonce, negative control, state checker, trajectory assertion, task tweak, or task-family slice is not a new platform-level research program by itself.
- **Gate padding:** another fail-closed label, threshold, preregistration wrapper, classifier or replay schema that does not change tomorrow's real decision is not an answer.
- **Bench substitution:** SWE-bench Verified and SWE-Bench Pro are not unquestioned replacements. OpenAI reported material flaws in 59.4% of a failure-enriched 138-task Verified subset and later estimated ~30% broken SWE-Bench Pro tasks; neither percentage applies to every task. MLCommons serving replay/inline accuracy does not establish final coding-job correctness.
- **Cross-host transfer:** throughput or speedups from CUDA/H100/other vendor stacks are not evidence about this pinned Mac. No new proposal may move MUST or the existing <=10-second and one-resident-model gates.

## Threshold for a new idea

Identify an implemented mechanism in at least one external project or lab with a pinned source revision or primary URL. Explain the user decision it would enable *that the current localbench CLI/packet/61-Bead graph cannot*, the concrete new product surface or closed loop it adds, and why it cannot be a one-line existing-Bead refinement. Specify a prior failure or realistic positive/negative scenario; demonstrate the smallest same-host known-good and known-bad test that would distinguish the mechanism from existing read/edit/mem/paired/SWE tests. State external evidence versus unverified Mac transfer. If this cannot be shown, mark the idea REJECTED-FOR-NOVELTY, not measured REFUTED.
